The Investment Forum is South Africa's longest-standing independent investment thought leadership conference, bringing together leading financial advisors, investment managers and industry thinkers.

In 2027, The Investment Forum explores Dire Straits. Hidden Order: Chaos is Rarely Random. At a time when markets are being shaped simultaneously by geopolitics, technology, debt, energy, shifting capital flows and changing investor behaviour, the world can often feel increasingly fragmented and difficult to interpret. But beneath the headlines, deeper forces are at work.

The Hidden Order is about uncovering the relationships between those forces, understanding not only what is happening, but what is driving it, what it influences and where the consequences may emerge next.

Across two days of thought-provoking discussions, The Investment Forum 2027 will move beyond isolated events to reveal the systems shaping markets, risk and opportunity. Because in investing, what appears to be chaos is rarely random.

Key Subject Matter

Five Chapters of
the Hidden Order

  • The global investment landscape is being reshaped by forces that extend far beyond markets alone. Geopolitics, trade, history and national interests are redefining the balance of economic power. Understanding where the world is heading begins with understanding how it is being reordered.
  • Capital has always told the real story. As markets evolve and private capital grows in influence, understanding where money is flowing and why, provides one of the clearest signals of where future opportunities may emerge.
  • Technology is no longer simply an industry; it has become the infrastructure upon which modern economies are being rebuilt. From Artificial Intelligence to semiconductors and digital assets, the next investment cycle will be shaped by the systems powering innovation.
  • Markets may evolve, but human behaviour rarely does. Risk, emotion and psychology continue to influence decision making as much as data and analysis. Understanding both the measurable and the behavioural dimensions of investing has never been more important.
  • Every period of disruption creates new leaders, new industries and new sources of growth. As capital, innovation and policy reshape the global economy, this chapter explores where tomorrow's investment opportunities are quietly taking shape.
